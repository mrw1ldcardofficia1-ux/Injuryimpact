Injuries are from club press conferences as reported by Rotowire, Fantasy Football Scout, Fantasy Football Hub and Squawka on 21 to 22 October 2026. Fixtures and win chances are from Sportradar. Only players whose club could be confirmed are listed. Ages are as of 22 October 2026. Nationality is the senior national team (Femi Azeez is listed as English but is also reported as Nigerian, so check which side he represents). Past injuries are from memory, so check them before publishing. “est.” weeks are estimates and the importance rating is my own judgement. Wages are unofficial weekly estimates in pounds, checked on 22 October 2026. Where Capology figures were available they are used and labelled; otherwise they come from SalarySport, so the two sources can differ. Pep Chavarría, Zadok Yohanna and Femi Azeez are shown without a wage because only outdated or placeholder figures exist. Contract end dates assume 30 June of the final year. “Contract value left” is his weekly wage multiplied by the weeks left on his deal, so it falls by one week’s wage for every week he is out.
