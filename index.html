      How the score works
      • Importance mixes how much he plays (share of available minutes) with his goals and assists per 90.
      • Replacement scales importance: easy x0.6, some drop-off x1, big drop-off x1.4.
      • Games missed comes from the injury's typical weeks out, at roughly one league game per week, capped at the games left.
      • Impact Score rises with importance x replacement x games missed, and levels off towards 10.
      • Points at risk is a rough guess at league points lost while he is out.

      It is a simple model, not a forecast. Real recovery depends on the player, the club's medical team and setbacks.
