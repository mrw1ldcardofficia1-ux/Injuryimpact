          “Out” is the number of injured or doubtful players in this tracker.

          Injury score (0 to 10) combines how long a player is out with how important he is to his team. Higher means a bigger blow. A club's score is the sum of its players' scores.

          Coverage: Premier League clubs with a published injury report are listed (Manchester City, Everton, Fulham, Crystal Palace and Coventry City had none found, so a missing club does not mean nobody is hurt). Championship and League One come from club and news reports published 10 to 22 October 2026 (soccernews.com, Championship Insights, The72, the Chad and Last Word On Football). League One is only partly covered and no current League Two injury reports were found, so those lists are incomplete. Wages are not shown for the three lower leagues. Nationalities were filled in from memory; ones I was less sure of say so when you open the card. Return times in the lower leagues are estimates, and some source dates looked stale, so treat them as a guide.

          Impact calculator: Impact Score = 40% minutes share + 30% share of team goals and assists + 20% how hard he is to replace + 10% difficulty of the fixtures he misses (weights from the original calculator, v1.0). Missing numbers count as zero, so add minutes and goals + assists for a full score. Games missed use the Premier League fixtures loaded here, then about one game a week. Time-out ranges for injury types are rough typical values. Injury-prone points and the Impact Score are estimates. Independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League or any club. Not a betting model. League table after 7 games each as of 20 October 2026 (Sky Sports and Tribuna). Managers are from the Premier League’s 2026/27 list, so check for changes after 22 October.

          This is a snapshot, not a live feed. Edit the DATA and FIX arrays in the script to refresh it. Injuries are from club press conferences as reported by Rotowire, Fantasy Football Scout, Fantasy Football Hub and Squawka on 21 to 22 October 2026. Fixtures and win chances are from Sportradar.

          Only players whose club could be confirmed are listed. Ages are as of 22 October 2026. Nationality is the senior national team (Femi Azeez is listed as English but is also reported as Nigerian, so check which side he represents). Past injuries are from memory, so check them before publishing. “est.” weeks are estimates and the importance rating is my own judgement. Wages are unofficial weekly estimates in pounds, checked on 22 October 2026. Where Capology figures were available they are used and labelled; otherwise they come from SalarySport, so the two sources can differ. Pep Chavarría, Zadok Yohanna and Femi Azeez are shown without a wage because only outdated or placeholder figures exist. Contract end dates assume 30 June of the final year. “Contract value left” is his weekly wage multiplied by the weeks left on his deal, so it falls by one week’s wage for every week he is out. Not medical advice.
