          “Out” is the number of injured or doubtful players in this tracker.

          Injury score (0 to 10) combines how long a player is out with how important he is to his team. Higher means a bigger blow. A club's score is the sum of its players' scores.

          Coverage: Premier League injuries are from Sportsgambler (updated 2 November 2026), with Mirror Football (30 October) and Fantasy Football Scout (29 October and 2 November) for detail. Suspensions are not listed. Everton, Fulham and Ipswich had no injuries reported, so a missing club does not mean nobody is hurt. Championship lists come from Starting11 and Sportsgambler (1 to 3 November); the two sources disagree in places and only give surnames, so those lists are best-effort, and return times are rough estimates. League One has only three long-term absences carried over from late October because no current reports were found, and League Two has none. Wages are not shown for the three lower leagues. Nationalities were filled in from memory; ones I was less sure of say so when you open the card.

          Impact calculator: Impact Score = 40% minutes share + 30% share of team goals and assists + 20% how hard he is to replace + 10% difficulty of the fixtures he misses (weights from the original calculator, v1.0). Missing numbers count as zero, so add minutes and goals + assists for a full score. Games missed use the Premier League fixtures loaded here, then about one game a week. Time-out ranges for injury types are rough typical values. Injury-prone points and the Impact Score are estimates. Independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League or any club. Not a betting model. League table after 9 games each as of 3 November 2026 (Sportradar). Managers are from the Premier League’s 2026/27 list as of 22 October, so check for changes.

          This is a snapshot, not a live feed. Edit the DATA and FIX arrays in the script to refresh it. Fixtures, results and win chances are from Sportradar. Last refreshed on 3 November 2026.

          Only players whose club could be confirmed are listed. Ages are as of 22 October 2026 and are only filled in for players carried over from the first version. Nationality is the senior national team (Femi Azeez is listed as English but is also reported as Nigerian, so check which side he represents). Past injuries are from memory, so check them before publishing. “est.” weeks are estimates and the importance rating is my own judgement. Wages are unofficial weekly estimates in pounds, checked on 22 October 2026. Wages are only shown for players carried over from the first version; newly listed players show “Wage n/a”. Where Capology figures were available they are used and labelled; otherwise they come from SalarySport, so the two sources can differ. Zadok Yohanna and Femi Azeez are shown without a wage because only outdated or placeholder figures exist. Contract end dates assume 30 June of the final year. “Contract value left” is his weekly wage multiplied by the weeks left on his deal, so it falls by one week’s wage for every week he is out. Not medical advice.
